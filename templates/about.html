What it does

{{ name }} helps small traders see whether they are making a profit or a loss, for each item and for the whole business.

  • Add your items with their buying and selling prices
  • Record how much you bought and how much you sold
  • See profit or loss per item, unsold stock, and the overall result
  • Add business expenses such as rent, transport and wages
  • Export a report to Excel, PDF, Word or CSV
How the calculation works
  • Sales = quantity sold × selling price
  • Cost = quantity bought × buying price + extra costs
  • Profit or loss = sales − cost
  • Unsold stock = quantity bought − quantity sold
  • Overall result = profit or loss of all items − business expenses
Part of Mezaneyo Online Shop (MOS)

{{ name }} is the profit-and-loss tool of Mezaneyo Online Shop (MOS), an e-business that sells products online and accepts mobile money payments. Shop owners use it to check how each product and the whole business is performing.
