In short

Your business figures stay on your own device. The app has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking.

What the app stores

The items, prices, quantities, expenses and settings you enter. They are saved in a database file on your phone or computer, so you can use them again later.

What we do not do
  • We do not collect your name, phone number, location or contacts
  • We do not send your business data to any server
  • We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use analytics or advertising
Internet and third-party content

The app's page design (Bootstrap) is loaded from a public content delivery network when you are online, so that network can see your internet address like any website. Your business data is never sent with that request.

Exports

When you export a report, the file is created on your device. You decide where to save it and who to share it with.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Clear all data, or uninstall the app, to remove everything.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
